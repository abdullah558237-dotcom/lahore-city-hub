Frequently Asked Questions

How are payments processed?

In this phase, all promotion campaigns are coordinated directly by our corporate partnerships desk. No payment details or live credit card charges are collected online. Invoicing is provided through official business channels.

Does paid promotion guarantee Google rankings?

No. Directory promotions enhance your visibility exclusively within the Lahore City Hub search and category index. We strictly avoid false ranking claims and comply with Google Search and FTC advertising transparency guidelines.

How do I verify my business ownership?

You can submit a claim request via your Owner Dashboard with proof documents such as an SECP registration certificate, Chamber of Commerce membership, utility bill, or official company email.

Why do listings have a "Sponsored" label?

Transparency is core to our editorial mission. Clear demarcation of paid promotional spotlights from organic community directory entries protects consumer trust and ensures complete ethical transparency.
