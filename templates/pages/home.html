PUBLIC TRANSIT NETWORKS

Lahore Public Transit & Route Guides

Navigating the Orange Line Metro Train, Green Line Metrobus, and intercity bus terminals.

Orange Line Metro Train (OLMT)

26 Stations

Connects Ali Town to Dera Gujran across 27.1 km. Direct stops at Anarkali, Chauburji, Lakshmi, and Shalamar Gardens.

  • Hours: 06:00 AM – 10:00 PM Daily
  • Fare: PKR 20 – 40 (Distance Based)
View Station Map & Guide

Lahore Metrobus (Green Line)

27 Stations

Dedicated corridor connecting Gajjumata to Shahdara through Ferozepur Road, Kalma Chowk, MAO College, and Azadi Chowk.

  • Hours: 06:00 AM – 10:30 PM Daily
  • Fare: Flat PKR 30 via Metro Token / Card

Airport & Intercity Transit

Allama Iqbal Int'l (LHE)

Airport access via Ring Road; Daewoo Express Thokar terminal, Lahore Junction Railway Station, and Speedo feeder buses.

  • Ring Road: 15 mins to DHA / Cantt
