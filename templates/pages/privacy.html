LEGAL & DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated & Verified: November 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Lahore City Hub ("LCH", "we", "our") operates as a civic and commercial information portal for Lahore, Pakistan. We are dedicated to maintaining the highest ethical standards of user privacy, strictly safeguarding user identities, contact records, and uploaded business verification documents.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Records: When you register for a member account, we collect your name, email address, password hash, and optional home locality.
  • Business Verification Records: When claiming or submitting a business listing, we collect official contact numbers, corporate email addresses, and verification documents (e.g., utility bills, SECP records).
  • Public Reviews: Ratings and commentary submitted on directory entities are published publicly alongside your member display name.
  • Technical Telemetry: Standard server logs, IP addresses, and anonymized analytics for load-balancing and DDOS protection.

3. How Information Is Utilized

We process data exclusively to verify listing legitimacy, prevent fraudulent review astroturfing, maintain civic helpline reliability, and deliver requested email newsletters. We strictly do not sell, rent, or monetize personal user records with third-party advertisers.

4. Document Security for Business Claims

Proof documents uploaded during the business claim process are stored in non-public, restricted directories accessible only by authorized staff moderators and auditors.

5. Inquiries & Data Rights

To request data deletion, review correction, or privacy clarifications, please email our Data Privacy Officer at privacy@lahorecityhub.pk.
