Overview & Cultural Significance

{{ item.summary }}

Commissioned during the zenith of the Mughal architectural renaissance, this monument reflects monumental proportions, red sandstone craftsmanship, and delicate marble inlays that continue to anchor Lahore's cultural identity. Visitors frequently describe the atmosphere during sunset as an unparalleled spiritual and aesthetic experience.

Visitor Guidelines & Accessibility

Photography: Mobile photography permitted free of charge.
Accessibility: Ramped entrance available at main gateway.
Shoe Custody: Token-based custodial counter at entrance.
Transit Shuttle: Electric shuttle connects to Delhi Gate.
